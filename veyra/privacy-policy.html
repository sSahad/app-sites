VEYRA

Speak languages with AI

Privacy Policy  ·  Effective 28 November 2026

The short version. Veyra's AI is Apple's on-device model, so your conversations are created on your iPhone or iPad and never leave it. There is no Veyra account and no Veyra server, and we never see what you say.

What does leave your device. The free version shows ads from Google, following the consent choices you make, and sends anonymous usage and crash reports. The reports are on by default; you can turn them off in Settings → Privacy & data → Share anonymous usage & crash reports. Veyra Plus removes the ads. Nothing you say to your companion is ever part of either.

Speech. Speech is recognised on your device or, for some languages, by Apple. See section 2.

1. What stays on your device

Veyra is an iPhone and iPad app for practising spoken languages with an AI companion. Everything you create or say in it is processed on your device by Apple's on-device models and stored only on your device:

  • Your spoken and typed conversations, transcripts and history.
  • The memory your companion keeps about you — your goals, vocabulary, corrections, streaks, level and settings.
  • Custom characters, avatars and any photo you use to create a look. Photos used for a look are not kept by Veyra and are never uploaded.
  • Translations of words and explanations into your language, made by Apple's on-device Translation framework and cached on your device.

We run no servers for Veyra, so we cannot read, copy or recover this data. Delete all data in Settings erases it from the device. Deleting the app erases it too.

2. Speech, microphone and on-device intelligence

Conversations are generated by Apple's on-device model. Speech recognition runs on your device or, for some languages, by Apple: iOS may process speech on Apple's servers when the on-device language asset is not installed. That processing is done by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, not by us.

Microphone and speech-recognition access are requested the first time you start a conversation, and each can be refused or revoked in iOS Settings. Veyra never records in the background.

Some features download assets from Apple the first time you use them (speech models, premium voices, translation language packs). Those downloads are requests to Apple and contain none of your content.

3. No account

Veyra does not ask you to sign up. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts, except what you choose to put in feedback you send us (section 4a).

4. Information collected by third-party services

The free version of Veyra shows ads and collects anonymous usage and crash information through Google services. Apart from feedback you choose to send us (section 4a), this is the only data that leaves your device, and conversation content is never part of it.

4a. Feedback you choose to send

In Settings you can open “Send feedback” and write to us. Only if you tap Send, we receive: your message (up to 1,000 characters), the email address you add if you want a reply (optional), the app version, iOS version, device model, app language and the language you practise. It never includes your conversations or what Veyra learned about you. It reaches us by email (through your Mail app) or, if we set one up, through a Google Form whose answers are stored in our Google account. We use it only to answer you and improve Veyra, keep it no longer than needed for that, and delete it on request. Analytics records only that feedback was sent and its kind (I love / something is wrong / I wish), never its text.

5. Tracking and your choices

  • Tracking permission. Veyra asks before using the advertising identifier for personalised ads. If you decline, ads are not personalised. Change it any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad consent. Where required, you see a consent form. Reopen it in Veyra under Settings → Privacy & data → Ad privacy options.
  • Analytics and crash reports. Turn them off in Veyra under Settings → Privacy & data → Share anonymous usage & crash reports. They are on by default until you do. When off, nothing is logged or queued.
  • In-app questions. The two short questions are optional rows on the screen; ignoring them is fine, and they stop after being ignored twice. They are hidden when Tips are off (Settings → App & about).
  • Remove ads. Veyra Plus removes ads entirely.

6. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store your payment details. Veyra checks your subscription status on your device through Apple's StoreKit.

7. Widgets, Siri and Shortcuts

The Veyra widget, Live Activity and Siri shortcuts read a small progress summary that the app shares with them on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Siri requests you make are handled by Apple under Apple's terms.

8. Notifications

Practice reminders are scheduled on your device and only if you allow notifications. They can be turned off in Veyra's Settings or in iOS Settings at any time.

9. Children

Veyra is not directed to children. Companion characters with romantic themes require a one-time confirmation that you are 17 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Your rights

Apart from feedback you sent us, we do not hold your personal data, so there is nothing else for us to access, correct or delete on our side. To have feedback you sent deleted, write to appwells31@gmail.com. For data held by Google (ads, analytics), use the controls above or Google's privacy tools. If you are in the EEA, UK or California and have a request, write to appwells31@gmail.com and we will explain what applies.

11. Security and retention

Your data stays in your device's protected storage. Analytics and crash data are retained by Google according to its default settings.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
