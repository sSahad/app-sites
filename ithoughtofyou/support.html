How it works

Think of someone

Each person in your Sky is a glowing orb. Hold their orb until it fills, then let go. They'll know you thought of them. There's no message to write. That's the point.

Add people to your Sky

Search for someone by name or username and open their profile. Tap Add to my Sky, or just hold their orb to think of them, which adds them too. Your Sky is yours alone. Adding someone doesn't add you to theirs.

Find someone in your Sky

Drag to move around your Sky and pinch to zoom. Tap someone in search, or choose Show in my Sky on their profile, and the Sky moves to them.

Frequently asked questions

Why can I think of someone only once a day?

So it stays special. A thought means the most when it's one moment, not a stream. You can think of the same person again tomorrow.

Is there a limit on how many people I can think of?

You can think of up to 50 different people in a day. That's plenty for real moments and keeps the app free of spam.

Will they see that it was me?

Yes. Thoughts are always named in the app. Notifications never show a name, though, so nothing personal appears on a lock screen.

How do I change my name, bio or photo?

Open Profile. Tap Edit profile to change your name and bio, or Add photo, Change photo or Remove photo for your picture.

The app can't access my photos

You may have refused photo access earlier. On iPhone, open Settings → I Thought of You → Photos. On Android, open Settings → Apps → I Thought of You → Permissions. Then try again.

How do I stop someone from reaching me?

Open their profile and tap Block. You won't see each other in search or profiles, and their thoughts won't reach you. They aren't told.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Delete account and confirm. Your account, profile, photo, thoughts, Sky and blocks are deleted immediately and permanently. See the privacy policy for details.

Something isn't working

  • Nothing loads. Check your internet connection, then close and reopen the app.
  • “You already thought of them today”. Try again tomorrow.
  • Can't find someone. Try their username, or the start of their first or last name. People who blocked you, or whom you blocked, don't show up.
