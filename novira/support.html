Getting Started

Novira works entirely on your device with no account or sign-up required. Open the app, complete the short onboarding, and start tracking. Here are a few things to explore first:

  • Log your period — Tap the Log Period button on the Dashboard to record your cycle start or end date
  • Set up pill reminders — Go to the Pill Tracker tab to select your pill brand and set a daily reminder time
  • Enable AI insights — Visit Settings to turn on personalized AI-powered health insights
  • Secure your data — Enable biometric or PIN lock in Settings → Security

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data private?

Yes. All your data is stored locally on your device and never leaves your phone. We have no servers, no accounts, and no access to your information. Read our full Privacy Policy for details.

How do I back up my data?

Go to Settings → Export Data to save a JSON backup of all your tracking data. You can store this file wherever you choose. Restoring from backup is not currently supported.

How accurate are the predictions?

Novira uses advanced statistical algorithms that improve over time as you log more cycles. Predictions are estimates based on your personal history and should not be used as a substitute for medical advice or contraception.

What does the AI feature do?

When enabled, Novira sends anonymized cycle phase information to generate personalized health insights, daily tips, and chat responses. No personally identifiable information is ever transmitted.

How do I delete all my data?

Go to Settings → Delete All Data. This permanently erases everything and cannot be undone. Alternatively, uninstalling the app removes all data automatically.

Can I change the app language?

Yes. Novira supports multiple languages. Go to Settings to select your preferred language.

Troubleshooting

  • Notifications not working — Make sure notifications are enabled for Novira in your device’s system settings. On iOS, go to Settings → Notifications → Novira
  • Predictions seem off — Novira needs at least 2–3 logged cycles to generate reliable predictions. Continue logging for improved accuracy
  • App feels slow — Try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, please contact us with your device model and iOS version
