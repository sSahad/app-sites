Shoot Shoot

AR shooting range in your room

Privacy Policy  ·  Effective 26 November 2026

The short version.

  • The game has no account, no sign-up and no server of ours. We do not receive your data.
  • The camera shows your room so targets can hang on your walls. The camera picture is processed on your iPhone. It is never recorded and never uploaded.
  • Your scores, saved ranges and settings stay on your iPhone.
  • If you watch an optional rewarded video, or a banner ad shows on the cooling-break screen between strings, Google AdMob (Google) collects some data to show and measure the ad. Players in regions that require it are asked for consent first.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. Scores and achievements go to Apple Game Center if you are signed in.

1. Who we are

Shoot Shoot (“the game”) is an iPhone game. You can reach us at appwells31@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your iPhone

  • Camera. The game uses the camera through Apple's ARKit and RealityKit to show your room and to place targets on walls, floors and furniture. The pictures are processed on the device while you play. They are not recorded, not saved and not sent anywhere.
  • Saved ranges. To bring a range back in the same room, the game saves a map of your room (an Apple ARKit world map) and the layout of the targets in the game's own storage on your iPhone. It never leaves the phone, except in a duel as described below.
  • Scores and settings. Your runs, personal bests, saved ranges and settings are stored on your iPhone. They are included in your own iPhone or iCloud device backup if you have one turned on.
  • Diagnostic log. The game keeps a short text log of its last ten sessions in its own storage on the iPhone (game events, timings, frame rate, heat state, and the counts in your rounds wallet). It contains no camera pictures, no name, no email address and no account identifier. It never leaves your iPhone unless you choose Settings, Data, Share and send it yourself, for example to ask for help. Detailed developer tracing and test tools are compiled only into development builds, not into the App Store version. Apple's own system logs stay on your device.
  • Deleting it. Settings, Data, “Delete runs and saved ranges” erases your scores, personal bests and saved ranges. Deleting the game removes everything it stored on the iPhone.

3. Rounds, purchases and iCloud backup

Rounds are the game's ammunition. You get free rounds every day, and you can watch an optional rewarded video for more rounds, buy rounds packs (consumable purchases), or make a one-time “Unlimited rounds” purchase.

  • Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, name or Apple Account. The game receives a confirmation from Apple that a purchase was made.
  • Restoring. The “Unlimited rounds” purchase can be restored on any iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account with Restore Purchases. Apple confirms the purchase to the game; we do not keep a record of it on a server.
  • Wallet. The game keeps a file on your iPhone with your round counts and the identifiers of past purchases, refunds and rewards.
  • iCloud backup of bought rounds. If you are signed in to iCloud, a copy of the record of your bought rounds (purchase identifiers, refunds and bought rounds used) is saved in your own iCloud key-value storage so that bought rounds come back if you reinstall the game or change iPhone. It is stored in your iCloud account under Apple's terms. We have no access to it. Free rounds are not part of it. If you sign in to a different iCloud account, the backup pauses and nothing is mixed between accounts.

4. Game Center

Game Center is optional. If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your best scores for the leaderboards and your achievements to Apple. Apple shows them to other players according to your Game Center settings and handles them under Apple's privacy policy. The game never asks you to sign in during play. If you do not sign in, nothing is sent.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game can offer a rewarded video, an optional video you watch to earn rounds. It is shown only when you tap to watch one, never during a string and never when the game starts.

During the cooling break between strings the break screen may also show one banner ad beside the countdown. The banner appears only on the cooling-break screen, never while you shoot, and any purchase permanently removes it.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob using Google's Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform.

  • What Google may collect. To deliver, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect device identifiers and similar data, information about the ads you see and how you interact with them, your approximate location derived from your IP address, and diagnostic information such as performance and crash data. Google is responsible for this data. See Google's explanation of how it uses data from apps that use its services and its privacy policy.
  • Non-personalised ads, no tracking prompt. The game asks Google for non-personalised ads. It does not show Apple's app tracking permission prompt, so your iPhone's advertising identifier is not available to the game or to Google through it.
  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and US states with their own rules, Google shows a consent message the first time you choose to watch a video, or once at the first cooling break that would show a banner. If you decline, you simply get no videos or banners and everything else in the game works. You can change your choice at any time under “Privacy choices” on the ROUNDS screen, where it is offered.

6. Duel (two iPhones in the same room)

Duel connects two iPhones that both have the game, over your local network using Apple's Multipeer Connectivity, with encryption required. iOS asks for Local Network permission the first time you look for the other phone. Nothing is sent to the internet or to any server.

  • The phones exchange shots, times and results, and a random identifier that the game creates on each iPhone. Your iPhone's name and your Apple Account are not sent.
  • So that both phones share the same range, the host's phone sends the target layout, the map of the host's room (an ARKit world map) and one small picture of the host's camera view (at most 480 pixels wide, used only to help the other player line up the phones) to the other phone. The guest's phone keeps these in memory for the duel only and does not save them. The host saves its own range map as for any saved range.
  • The picture and the map are sent from phone to phone only after both players join a duel, and only while the duel is being set up.

7. Photos

If you tap SNAPSHOT or use the hardware button as a snapshot button, the game saves that picture of your range to your Photos library. The game can only add pictures. It never reads your photos, and iOS asks your permission first.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children. It is rated 13+ on the App Store because it shows realistic-looking firearms on a target range. The game itself collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

9. Your rights and choices

We do not hold personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to look up, correct or delete on our side. You control the data that exists:

  • On your iPhone: delete scores and saved ranges in Settings, or delete the game.
  • Camera, Photos and Local Network access can be turned off at any time in the iPhone's Settings.
  • Ads: use “Privacy choices” in the game, and Google's ad settings.
  • Game Center and iCloud: manage them in the iPhone's Settings and Apple's privacy controls.
  • If you live in the EEA, the United Kingdom, California or elsewhere with data protection rights, you can ask Google (for ad data) or Apple (for Game Center, iCloud and purchases) to exercise them, or write to us at appwells31@gmail.com and we will help as far as we can.

10. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update it and change the date at the top. The current version is always at this address.
