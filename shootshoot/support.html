Getting started

Shoot Shoot is an AR shooting range for iPhone. Hang targets on your real walls and shoot them with your thumb.

Which iPhones does it work on?

Shoot Shoot needs an iPhone that supports ARKit and runs iOS 27.0 or later. An iPhone with LiDAR is recommended: it lets targets and drones understand your walls, floor and furniture. Other supported iPhones use plane detection, where targets still hang on walls but drones cannot hide behind furniture. It is iPhone only and plays in landscape.

Scan the room

Hold the iPhone in landscape and move it slowly across the walls and floor while the game maps your room. The camera needs light and some texture on the walls.

Place a target

Pick a range, tap where the first target should go, adjust the distance and press PLACE.

Aim and fire

Hold the iPhone with two hands. Aim by moving the phone, hold AIM with your left thumb and press FIRE with your right thumb. Left-handed players can switch the layout in Settings, Play, Hand.

Reload

Swipe down on the screen to reload.

Common problems

The game cannot see the room, or the camera is black

Point the iPhone at a lit wall, then the floor, and move slowly. Also check that the camera is allowed: iPhone Settings, Shoot Shoot, Camera. If access was denied, the game shows a button that opens Settings.

I cannot see a target or drone

On an iPhone with LiDAR, drones can hide behind furniture and walls. Move so you have a clear line to them.

“Not enough room here” with Clays

Clays need open space to fly. Step back or face the long side of the room and try again.

“Room too small to map” with Drone Swarm

Drones need free space to fly in. Try a larger room or clear some floor space and scan again.

The iPhone gets warm and the range goes cold

After a few strings the game gives the iPhone a short cooling break, counts it down and then the range is hot again. After thirty minutes of shooting it suggests a longer rest. This is on purpose and protects your phone.

No sound

Check Settings, Sound and Feel, Sound in the game, the volume and the ring/silent switch.

My saved range does not come back

A saved range comes back only in the same room where you saved it, because the game matches the saved map of the room. Stand in the same room, choose the saved range and look around slowly until the targets return. A range saved in one room does not restore in another.

Does the game record or upload my camera?

No. The camera picture is used on your iPhone to place targets. It is not recorded or uploaded. Read the full privacy policy.

Rounds and purchases

Rounds are the ammunition. You get free rounds every day, can watch an optional rewarded video for more, can buy rounds packs, or can make a one-time “Unlimited rounds” purchase. Any purchase permanently removes the banner ad. The foam blaster and Duel never use rounds, so you can always play.

Restore purchases

The “Unlimited rounds” purchase can be restored with Restore Purchases on the ROUNDS screen, on any iPhone signed in to the Apple Account that bought it. Rounds from packs are backed up to your iCloud, so they come back after a reinstall if you are signed in to the same iCloud account.

I bought rounds and they are missing

Open ROUNDS on the home screen and check the balance. If the ROUNDS screen says “iCloud backup: paused”, you are signed in to a different Apple Account than the one that bought them. Sign in with the original account and the backup resumes. If rounds still do not arrive, write to us at appwells31@gmail.com with the date of the purchase.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. A refunded purchase is taken back by the game.

The video did not play

Videos are optional and come from Google AdMob. A video is offered only when you ask for one and may be unavailable at times (no internet, no ad ready, or you declined the ad consent message). You can change that choice under “Privacy choices” on the ROUNDS screen. Nothing else in the game depends on videos.

Duel

Duel needs two iPhones that both have Shoot Shoot, in the same room on the same Wi-Fi or with Bluetooth on. Tap DUEL on the home screen on both phones, allow Local Network access when iOS asks, and join. If it cannot find the other phone, check iPhone Settings, Shoot Shoot, Local Network, and make sure both phones are in the same room. Duels never use rounds.

Other questions

How do I delete my data?

Settings, Data, “Delete runs and saved ranges” erases your scores and saved ranges from the iPhone. Deleting the game removes everything it stored on the phone. Game Center scores stay with Apple.

How do I send you a log?

Settings, Data, Share sends the newest diagnostic log through the share sheet. It holds game events and timings only, no camera pictures. Attach it to an email to appwells31@gmail.com.

Hardware buttons, controllers, Game Center, units

Settings, Controls turns on hardware buttons (they stay off until you run the button test there). Settings, Game Center is optional. Settings, Play has Units (metres or yards), Hand (right or left), Reduce effects and Hit feedback.

Safety

  • Look around you before you play and keep your eyes up while you move the phone.
  • Clear some space around you. You will turn and step while you hold the phone.
  • Play at home or somewhere safe. Never point the phone at people to “aim” at them.
  • The game uses virtual targets only. It uses realistic-looking firearms as sports equipment on a target range; there are no people or animals to shoot. Always treat real firearms with care.
